State of the League

Through Week 11 of the 2026 season, the top of the Complex League is a three-way tie at 8-3: Colin Stone, Daniel Grover, and John Koch. The Season Lucky Duck goes to Ryan Bartman, who won Week 1 with just 98.56 points.
