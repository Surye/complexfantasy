What Is This?

The Phantom League uses a two-game weekly format. Every team gets one result from its normal head-to-head matchup and one from the median game, where the top half of the week's scores earn a win and the bottom half a loss. A team can therefore finish a week 2-0, 1-1, or 0-2. Combined records are ranked by wins, then points for, then points against.
